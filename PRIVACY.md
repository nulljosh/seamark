# Seamark Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

Seamark is a chart reader. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What leaves your device
- **Your image.** A chart you upload is read to pull the numbers out, then thrown away.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Seamark isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
